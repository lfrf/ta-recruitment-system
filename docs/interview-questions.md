# Stakeholder Interview Questions

## TA Applicant
1. How do you currently find available TA opportunities?
2. Which part of the current application process is the most inconvenient?
3. What information do you expect to maintain in your applicant profile?
4. What concerns do you have when uploading a CV?
5. Which job details matter most before you decide to apply?
6. How would you like to view your application status?
7. Have you ever applied for multiple TA roles at the same time? If so, what became confusing?
8. If the system could save you time in one step, which step should it improve first?

## Module Organiser
9. What information do you need when posting a TA role?
10. Which criteria do you use most when screening applicants?
11. How do you currently keep track of multiple applicants and their status?
12. Which problem occurs most often during applicant review?
13. Do you need to see an applicant's existing workload before making a decision? Why?
14. What should the accept or reject workflow look like?
15. What makes a recruitment interface easy for you to use?
16. If the system gives a matching score, what explanation would you expect to see?

## Admin
17. How do you currently monitor the overall workload of TAs?
18. When do you consider a TA to be overloaded?
19. What summary information or reports would help you most?
20. Which system failures would have the biggest impact on administrative efficiency?
