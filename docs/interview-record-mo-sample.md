- Session date: 2026-03-11

## Questions and Answers

### Question 1
**Question:** What information must be included when posting a TA role?  
**Answer summary:** Job title, module, job description, required skills, expected workload, and deadline are essential.

### Question 2
**Question:** What applicant information is most useful during review?  
**Answer summary:** Relevant skills, CV, programme background, and availability are the most useful items.

### Question 3
**Question:** What is the hardest part of reviewing applicants?  
**Answer summary:** Comparing applicants is slow when their information, CVs, and status are stored separately.

### Question 4
**Question:** Do you need to see an applicant's workload before making a decision? Why?  
**Answer summary:** Yes. Existing workload matters because an applicant may already be committed to other roles.

### Question 5
**Question:** What would make accepting or rejecting applicants easier?  
**Answer summary:** A clear applicant list, visible profile details, and one-step decision recording would simplify the process.

## Key Findings
- MOs need a structured job posting form
- MOs need applicant information and CV access in one review flow
- MOs need consistent decision recording

## Pain Points
- manual comparison of applicants is inefficient
- tracking status across several files is inconvenient
- workload checking is not integrated into the review process

## Candidate Requirements

### Functional
- The system shall allow an MO to post a TA role.
- The system shall show applicants for a selected job.
- The system shall show relevant profile and CV information during review.
- The system shall allow the MO to accept or reject applicants.

### Non-functional
- Decision updates should be traceable.
- The review process should minimise unnecessary navigation.

## Backlog and Report Notes
- related user stories: post job, view applicants, review applicant details, accept or reject applicant
- useful points for the brief report: MO review needs efficiency, traceability, and better visibility of applicant information
- follow-up actions: make sure prototype review page shows job summary, applicant details, and decision controls together
