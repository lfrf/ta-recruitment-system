# Interview Record – Module Organiser Stakeholder

**Project:** TA Recruitment System  
**Stakeholder role:** Module Organiser  
**Interview type:** Semi-structured role-based discussion  
**Date:** 2026-03-10  
**Recorded by:** Group 27

## Summary of Discussion
The discussion suggested that Module Organisers need to define clear vacancy information when posting a TA role. Important items include the module name, job title, responsibilities, required skills, expected workload, and application deadline.

When reviewing applicants, MOs rely on profile information, CV content, and relevant experience or skills. The current manual process is inconvenient because information is often spread across forms, files, or spreadsheets, which makes comparison slower and less consistent.

MOs want a clearer way to review applicants for a selected role, compare their details efficiently, and record outcomes in a traceable way. They may also need visibility of workload information when deciding whether a candidate is already heavily committed elsewhere.

## Main Pain Points
- applicant information is split across multiple locations;
- manual comparison of applicants is slow and inconvenient;
- decisions are harder to trace when records are inconsistent.

## Derived Requirements
- The system shall allow an MO to create and publish a TA vacancy.
- The system shall allow an MO to view applicants for a selected vacancy.
- The system shall display relevant applicant profile information and CV reference during review.
- The system shall allow an MO to accept or reject an applicant and record the decision.
- The system should allow an MO to view workload information where relevant.
